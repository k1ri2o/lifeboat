## Lifeboat 0.1.17

**Make Lifeboat bigger, even when there is no free space behind it.** Before, Make bigger only worked if the drive had empty space right after the Lifeboat partition, and otherwise showed a confusing "can't grow here" message. Now Lifeboat can borrow space from the drive in front of it: it builds a new, bigger hidden partition, copies every backup into it, checks that the copy is complete and healthy, and only then removes the old one and gives its space back. Your password is needed. If anything goes wrong on the way, the old Lifeboat stays as it was. If the PC is switched off in the middle, Lifeboat finishes or undoes the move at the next start.

**Clearer drive numbers.** The used space on the "Your drives" card and on the Lifeboat card now agree, and each drive says how it is connected (SATA, NVMe, USB, ...).

Install over the old version. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**.
